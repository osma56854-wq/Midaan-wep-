# MIDAAN global platform roadmap

## Current platform foundation
- Factory workspaces, organization membership and roles
- Skills library with evidence uploads and review workflow
- Machine register, fault reports and recurring preventive maintenance
- Professional profiles and industrial service listings
- Arabic/English interface foundation with RTL/LTR switching
- Light/dark themes with high-contrast component tokens
- Industrial RFQs with supplier quotes and buyer award workflow

## Next product milestones
1. Organization invitations, member administration and technician assignment
2. Industrial catalog: machines, spare parts, raw materials and consumables
3. Supplier/company verification, credentials and document review
4. Ratings and reviews after completed service or awarded order
5. Private project messaging and notifications
6. Order/contract milestones, delivery evidence and dispute handling
7. International localization: country directory, currencies, time zones, date/number formats
8. Moderation, abuse reporting, admin console, audit trail and privacy exports

## Launch principles
- Free-tier infrastructure only until validated demand justifies a change.
- Never represent an unverified supplier as verified.
- No payment escrow, shipping guarantees or compliance claims until appropriate providers, legal terms and operational processes exist.
- Public listings must not expose personal contact details, private factory records or proprietary drawings.
- Keep RLS and least-privilege database grants as the source of truth for access control.
- Validate every critical workflow with at least two independent authenticated accounts before launch.

## Release status
The current branch implements the global RFQ workflow and accessibility/language improvements. It is a product foundation, not yet a production-complete global marketplace.
